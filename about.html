01 — Biography

Biography

I’m in the second year of an MSc in Financial Data Intelligence at Rennes School of Business, after a degree in Business and Management at ENCG El Jadida in Morocco covering accounting, management control, financial analysis and corporate finance. What draws me to the field is a practical question: how do we make financial decisions more rigorous when the data is noisy, incomplete and constantly changing?

I first met that question at Bank of Africa, where I produced weekly management reports, checked the integrity of transaction data for daily reconciliation and monitored client accounts for anomalies. Since then I’ve built projects that tackle it directly: QuantRisk Analytics, a tested portfolio risk tool, a fraud analysis of 6.36 million transactions, and an interactive primer on volatility. I also write short educational articles to practise explaining technical ideas clearly.

I’m looking for a 6-month end-of-studies internship in risk, quantitative or financial data analysis, starting in late March 2027, where I can contribute careful analytical work and learn from experienced practitioners.
